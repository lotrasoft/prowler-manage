New instance

Installs the latest Prowler App release, connects it to the tenant and publishes it through Cloudflare Tunnel.

Set up your MSP app once first. It's a multi-tenant app in your own tenant that each customer approves.

The customer approves your Prowler app once through Microsoft's admin-consent page. That creates the enterprise app in their tenant.

  • Approve now: sign in as the customer's Global Administrator, or with your partner account through GDAP (Global Administrator, or Privileged Role Administrator + Cloud Application Administrator).
  • Or just click Create: the instance installs and shows a consent link to send to the customer, then continues on its own once they approve.

    Sign in as a Global Administrator of the customer's tenant. The manager creates a "Prowler" app registration there with an auto-renewing certificate, then Microsoft asks you to approve Prowler's read-only permissions.

      The manager generates a certificate. You upload it to the app registration once; after that it renews itself before it expires. For self-renewal the app also needs the Graph application permission Application.ReadWrite.OwnedBy and must be an owner of its own registration. The exact steps appear while the instance is created.

      The app registration needs the permissions listed in the README (Microsoft Graph, Exchange Online, Teams, and the Global Reader role) with admin consent.
